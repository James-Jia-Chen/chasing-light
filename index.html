AN IMPORTANT LITTLE NOTE

The beauty is still up to the sky.

Our photo potential scores are creative estimates, not professional forecasts. We look at cloud cover, the chance of rain, wind, and temperature at the real golden-hour window. A little cloud can make a dramatic sky; too much rain can make that perfect shot tricky.

How does the score work? +

We combine a cloud-cover preference (34 points, favoring partial clouds), dry conditions (38), lighter wind (16), and comfortable temperature (12). Severe forecast weather can reduce the score. We sample the nearest forecast hours around the middle of each golden-hour period. Scores are for inspiration, not safety or photographic certainty.
